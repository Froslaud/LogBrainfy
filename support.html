Account Deletion

Users have full control over their data within LogBrainfy. All areas of the application—including notes, tasks, projects, habits, areas, and lists—can be edited or deleted at any time directly within the app.

If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, you may contact us using the email below:

Request Account Deletion froslaud@gmail.com

Upon receiving your request, we will process the account deletion in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Once completed, all associated personal data and user-generated content will be permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required by law.

  • Data stored locally on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app or deleting content manually.
  • Cloud-synced data will be deleted from our servers after account deletion.
  • The deletion process is irreversible.
